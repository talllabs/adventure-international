## Kilimanjaro & Northern Tanzania: The Icon, Elevated

For many travelers, standing on the summit of Mount Kilimanjaro is the ultimate goal. What we offer isn't simply a way to get there — it's a way to experience the mountain in genuine comfort and style, with expert planning shaping every step.

Our signature Kilimanjaro experience is designed around extended routes and generous acclimatization schedules, allowing guests to adapt naturally and move through the mountain's changing landscapes at the right pace.

Supported throughout by highly trained guides and one of the best-equipped teams on the mountain, the emphasis is always on wellbeing, preparation, and enjoyment. Spacious guest tents with proper bed cots and pillows, private showers and toilets, and comfortable dining tents ensure that guests remain cared for from the rainforest all the way to the summit.

The result is an ascent that feels both achievable and deeply rewarding, even for first-time climbers.

And after Kilimanjaro, we seamlessly shift the focus from peaks to plains with one of our most distinctive experiences: the biking safari.

We were pioneers of this style of exploration more than a decade ago and remain the only operators offering e-bikes on safari. Our biking journeys take guests deep into northern Tanzania's most wildlife-rich regions, combining mountain biking with traditional game drives for a far more intimate and active way of exploring these breathtaking landscapes.

Riding through the greater Tarangire ecosystem, along the Great Rift Valley, and toward Lake Manyara, travelers experience Tanzania as active participants — noticing more, connecting more deeply, and engaging with the environment in a way few safaris allow.

For adventurous travelers wanting to experience Tanzania beyond the expected, it's a pairing that feels both bold and beautifully natural.

## Cotopaxi & The Galapagos: From Ice to Equator

For travelers looking to take the next step after Kilimanjaro, Cotopaxi offers the perfect progression.

Rising dramatically along Ecuador's Avenue of the Volcanoes, Cotopaxi introduces guests to true alpine mountaineering, where crampons, ice axes, and rope systems become part of the journey.

With Adventure International, the climb is carefully designed as another thoughtful progression beneath the expansive skies of the Andes.

And for travelers who prefer less time on the mountain, Ecuador's mainland offers exceptional trekking and multi-sport opportunities, paired with thoughtfully selected lodges where guests can recover in comfort — often soaking tired muscles in nearby hot springs after active days.

From colonial Quito, often considered the cultural capital of South America, it's easy to continue onward to the Galapagos Islands.

Our approach to the Galapagos is intentionally different from standard cruise-led itineraries.

Rather than skimming the surface, we create privately guided, carefully curated island-hopping experiences by land and sea, with guests staying in luxury island hotels instead of spending the entire trip aboard a ship. This creates a more grounded connection with local life while allowing more time immersed in the archipelago's remarkable ecosystems.

Days unfold through activity and exploration — snorkeling at iconic sites like Kicker Rock and La Loberia, hiking volcanic terrain across Isabela and Santa Cruz, kayaking around Tintoreras Island, biking scenic landscapes, and encountering sea lions, penguins, giant tortoises, and marine turtles along the way.

Together, Ecuador and the Galapagos create a beautifully balanced adventure for travelers wanting both progression and immersion in one of the world's most biodiverse destinations.

## Everest Base Camp & Babai River: High Himalayas to Deep Jungle

The journey to Everest Base Camp is legendary, but our version goes far beyond the standard route.

After flying into Lukla and acclimatizing through Namche Bazaar — where luxury lodges offer comfort away from the trekking crowds — guests venture deeper into the hidden valleys of the Khumbu, exploring lesser-visited regions including Khangtaiga, Mingbo La, Imja, and Ama Dablam valleys.

In these remote areas, we establish our fully equipped VIP Yarin Camp, where guests enjoy remarkable comfort and solitude supported by expert guides, porters, camp staff, and exceptional culinary teams.

And of course, there is Everest Base Camp itself.

Guests helicopter into the region to explore Base Camp and surrounding high-altitude lakes including Imja, Gokyo, and Scoundrel's Viewpoint, experiencing the immense scale of the Himalayas without adding unnecessary physical strain.

Then comes a dramatic shift in environment.

From the high Himalayas, we guide travelers into the subtropical lowlands of western Nepal and our proprietary Babai River Camp — a luxury wilderness camp positioned deep within one of the Indian subcontinent's last true wildlife refuges.

Here, Asiatic elephants graze freely, one-horned rhinoceros roam the floodplains, crocodiles patrol the waterways, and Royal Bengal tigers still move through the forests.

Guided by expert naturalists, guests experience this extraordinary ecosystem in deeply immersive ways, creating a powerful contrast to the high-altitude adventure that came before it.
